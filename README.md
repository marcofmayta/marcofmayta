## About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the Institute of Mathematics and Computer Sciences (ICMC), University of São Paulo, advised by Prof. Alneu de Andrade Lopes. My research interests include graph machine learning, complex networks, efficient and reliable AI, and quantum computing.

My background is in Statistical and Computer Engineering at Universidad Nacional del Altiplano, Peru. I also completed an MBA in Artificial Intelligence and Big Data at ICMC-USP.

I develop computational research with an emphasis on rigorous evaluation, reproducibility, and practical constraints.

## Research interests

- **Graph machine learning:** graph neural networks, graph representations, topology, and robustness.
- **Complex networks:** spectral methods, community structure, and network analysis.
- **Efficient and reliable AI:** resource-aware learning, numerical robustness, and reproducible benchmarking.
- **Quantum computing:** quantum optimization, circuit transpilation, and hybrid approaches to machine learning.
- **Applied machine learning:** biomedical applications, cybersecurity, and predictive modeling.

## Selected work

- **[Quantum Malware Intelligence](https://github.com/marcofmayta/quantum-malware-intelligence)** — Reproducible malware-classification benchmarking with classical ML, class-imbalance handling, feature selection, and quantum-ready experiments.
- **[QAOA in Practice](https://marcofmayta.github.io/QAOA/)** — Educational project connecting quantum approximate optimization algorithms with mathematical formulations and code.
